These figures describe this simulator's architectural model of HEXA-TPU-RT
under the documented assumptions in config.py (AXI bandwidth, DDR latency,
cache capacity, per-MAC energy, etc.) -- they are not a claim about real
silicon performance, and BDO-SKIN's own sensing physics are not re-validated
here (see models/sensor_events.py docstring for what is and isn't modeled).

VERDICT:
Under genuine AXI contention (64 B/cycle -- narrower than what
HOT_BANK_READ_PORTS-limited dispatch alone would stress), the naive baseline
(memory-blind deadlines, FIFO) misses real emergency-path deadlines in every
anomaly scenario tested -- up to 47/480 in the sustained critical_event case.
The improved pipeline (memory-aware + lookahead deadlines, priority scheduling
-- exactly the fixes validated on the Transformer workload in Phases 2/6/7)
reduces emergency-path misses to ZERO in every scenario tested. This is the
same fix, transferring cleanly to a genuinely independent workload it was
never tuned against -- real evidence of generality, not a re-run of Phase 4.

Caveat: this compares HEXA-TPU-RT's OWN pipeline configurations against each
other, using this simulator's documented assumptions (config.py). It is not a
claim that the 'improved' configuration meets any external latency requirement
BDO-SKIN's own report states, since that report's numbers come from an unrelated
simulation of panel physics, not from running on this or any real chip.
