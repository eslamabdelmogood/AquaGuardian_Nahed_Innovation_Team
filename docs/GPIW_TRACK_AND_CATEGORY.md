# GPIW 2026 — Track and Category Recommendation

Based on the official *GPIW 2026 Applicant Guidelines* (provided by the applicant).

## Track: A — TRL 1–3 (Research & Proof of Concept)

The Guidelines define Track A as early-stage innovations that have "demonstrated a
scientific basis or proof of concept but have not yet undergone significant
validation in operational environments," evaluated on "scientific merit,
originality, transformative potential, and future scalability."

This matches AquaGuardian's own `docs/TRL_ASSESSMENT.md` classification exactly:
a working deterministic software prototype, digital-twin abstraction, automated
tests, and reproducible comparative evidence — explicitly **not** claiming field
deployment, hardware-in-the-loop validation, or utility integration.

**Do not apply under Track B.** The Guidelines' TRL-consistency check would flag a
TRL 4–7 declaration as inconsistent with the evidence on hand (no lab/pilot/field
validation exists yet), risking reclassification or a credibility hit with the
Technical Jury. Track A is both the honest and the strategically correct choice —
Track A projects are judged on the strength of the underlying idea and its
validation *path*, not on deployment proof AquaGuardian doesn't have.

## Category: Digitalisation, Automation and Smart Water Management

Of the six Track A/B categories, this is the only one where the Guidelines'
own description matches the project's *core* mechanism, not just a side benefit:

> "digital, automated, and data-driven innovations that improve the monitoring,
> operation, control, prediction, optimization, or management of water systems
> ... artificial intelligence, machine learning ... digital twins, predictive
> analytics, autonomous control systems ... decision-support tools"

AquaGuardian's differentiator — simulate, stress-test, and validate a decision
*before* execution — is a digital/AI decision-support mechanism first. The water
outcomes (leak containment, irrigation savings, fire-risk reduction) are what
that mechanism produces, not what it *is*.

**Category is single-select and exclusive** (Guidelines, Section 7): the same
project cannot also be submitted under Water Efficiency, Conservation and
Consumption Management or Environmental Sustainability. Use the Impact & Market
section of the application to surface those secondary benefits instead of
submitting a second application — the Guidelines explicitly prohibit resubmitting
the same project under a different category.

## Why not Track C

Track C (TRL 8–9 Special Mentions) requires demonstrated real-world adoption,
investment, or ecosystem impact. AquaGuardian has none of that yet — Track C
would fail Stage 1 administrative screening (TRL-consistency check) outright.
