# GPIW 2026 — Video Presentation Script (target: 90 seconds)

Required by Section 6 of the Guidelines: 1–2 minutes, MP4/MOV/AVI, max 50MB.
Timings are approximate — read at a natural pace and adjust.

---

**[0:00–0:12] Hook — the problem**

> "Water infrastructure is still reactive. Leaks are found after the water's
> already gone. Pumps fail before anyone's warned. Irrigation runs on a
> schedule, not on what the plant actually needs."

*(Optional: show the "Detect → React" struck-through line from the deck.)*

**[0:12–0:30] The idea**

> "AquaGuardian AI flips that loop. Before any action reaches a valve, a pump,
> or a drone, it's simulated, stress-tested, and validated in a digital twin.
> If a plan fails — say, closing a valve that would starve another sector —
> it's rejected, automatically re-optimized, and re-tested. Nothing executes
> until it's proven safe."

*(Show the closed-loop diagram: Sense → Analyze → Simulate → Stress Test →
Optimize → Validate → Execute.)*

**[0:30–0:55] The evidence**

> "This isn't a mockup. It's a working proof of concept: five water-risk
> scenarios, run under four stress profiles each, benchmarked against a
> reactive baseline. On average: seventy-four percent less water lost,
> response delay cut from five minutes to a quarter of a second, and a
> stress-test pass rate that goes from zero percent to one hundred percent.
> All of it reproducible — the numbers come from code in the repository, not
> a slide."

*(Show the live console: pick the leak scenario, let it run through the
stress-test rejection and re-optimization.)*

**[0:55–1:15] Honesty about where it is**

> "We're submitting this as what it is: a TRL 3 proof of concept. It hasn't
> been deployed in the field, and it doesn't control real infrastructure yet.
> What it does have is a defined path to TRL 4 — real sensors, calibration
> against measured behavior, fail-safe testing — and a solo founder's
> background across four connected projects in edge AI, embedded systems, and
> validation-before-action design."

**[1:15–1:30] The ask / close**

> "What we need next is a design partner — a farm, an irrigation district, a
> small utility — to run this in shadow mode and tell us where the model is
> wrong. That's what a GPIW pilot pathway is built for. Thank you."

## Production notes

- Screen-record the live console (`/mnt/user-data/outputs` build, or the
  hosted demo URL once available) for the 0:30–0:55 segment — it's stronger
  evidence than narration alone.
- Keep the founder's face or voice present at least at open and close;
  jurors are explicitly told to weigh "team capability and credibility."
- Do not claim field results, pilots, or partnerships that don't exist yet —
  the Guidelines' integrity screening and the Technical Jury's TRL-consistency
  check are both explicitly looking for exactly that kind of overreach.
