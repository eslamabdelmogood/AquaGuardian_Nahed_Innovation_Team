# Business Case

## Problem

Water operators often receive alarms after a threshold is crossed, but still need people to decide what action is safe. A valve closure, pump transfer or irrigation adjustment can solve one problem while creating another elsewhere in the network.

AquaGuardian addresses the gap between **detecting a problem** and **validating a response before it reaches physical infrastructure**.

## Beachhead market

The recommended first market is **commercial farms and managed irrigation districts in water-stressed regions**.

Why this segment first:

- water use and energy cost are measurable;
- pilots can be scoped to one pump station or irrigation zone;
- existing sensors can often be reused;
- decisions such as irrigation timing, pump loading and leak isolation have clear operational value;
- procurement is generally simpler than a city-wide utility deployment.

Municipal utilities, reservoirs and treatment plants are later expansion markets after field evidence and security certification.

## Customer value

AquaGuardian is designed to provide:

- earlier identification of leaks, drought stress and pump degradation;
- lower modeled water loss and avoidable service disruption;
- safer operational decisions through simulation and stress testing;
- an auditable decision trail for operators and regulators;
- local edge operation when cloud connectivity is unavailable.

## Commercial offering

### Pilot package

A fixed-scope shadow-mode pilot for one site or subsystem:

- sensor/data integration;
- digital-twin configuration;
- risk and action calibration;
- operator dashboard;
- validation report.

### Production model

A hybrid commercial model is proposed:

1. **Integration fee** for site modeling and commissioning.
2. **Annual software subscription** per monitored site or asset group.
3. **Optional edge gateway** when customers require local inference and offline resilience.
4. **Enterprise licensing and support** for utilities or infrastructure operators.

## Planning assumptions — not quotations

The following ranges are early commercial hypotheses to be tested through customer discovery:

| Item | Planning range |
|---|---:|
| Shadow-mode pilot | USD 10,000–30,000 |
| Site integration/commissioning | USD 15,000–60,000 |
| Annual software subscription | USD 6,000–24,000 per site |
| Optional edge gateway BOM | USD 350–900 per node |
| Enterprise support | Priced by assets, sites and SLA |

These figures are not market prices or signed offers. They are budgeting assumptions for pilot design.

## Why customers would pay

The purchase case is not “AI for water.” It is a reduction in the cost and risk of operational decisions. A pilot succeeds only when the customer can verify at least one of the following:

- reduced water loss;
- reduced pumping energy;
- avoided unplanned downtime;
- improved irrigation efficiency;
- faster incident containment;
- improved compliance and decision traceability.

## Commercial milestones

1. Obtain two design-partner interviews.
2. Secure one letter of interest for a shadow-mode pilot.
3. Validate one scenario using recorded or live sensor data.
4. Convert the pilot into a paid deployment.
5. Build repeatable integration templates for similar sites.
