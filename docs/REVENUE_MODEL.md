# Revenue Model

## Recommended model

AquaGuardian should be sold as a service-enabled software platform rather than as a one-time hardware product.

### Revenue streams

1. Paid feasibility or shadow-mode pilot.
2. Site integration and digital-twin configuration.
3. Recurring annual software subscription.
4. Optional edge gateway and sensor integration margin.
5. Enterprise support, training and service-level agreements.

## Unit of sale

The initial unit of sale is one monitored site or one bounded operational subsystem, such as:

- one pump station;
- one irrigation block;
- one pipeline pressure zone.

This keeps deployment value measurable and avoids an unrealistic full-utility sale at TRL 3.

## Pricing validation

All price ranges in the business case are hypotheses. They must be validated through customer interviews and at least one paid pilot. The preferred commercial metric is value relative to verified water, energy and downtime savings, not the number of AI calls or sensors.
