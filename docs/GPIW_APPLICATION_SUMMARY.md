# GPIW Application Summary

## Project

**AquaGuardian AI — Closed-Loop Edge AI for Resilient Water Infrastructure**

## Problem

Water infrastructure is mainly reactive: leaks, pump failures, contamination and drought stress are often handled after damage or water loss has already occurred. Existing monitoring tools may detect anomalies, but they do not systematically simulate and stress-test alternative responses before action.

## Solution

AquaGuardian AI creates a living digital twin at the edge. Sensor and plant bio-signal data are analyzed locally. Candidate operational decisions are simulated under nominal and adversarial conditions. Only decisions that pass safety, water-loss, contamination, fire-risk and service-continuity constraints are approved.

## Innovation

**Decision Before Execution:** every action is predicted, simulated, optimized, stress-tested and validated before it reaches physical infrastructure.

## PoC evidence

- Four water-risk scenarios
- Offline edge decision path
- Multi-action digital-twin comparison
- Adversarial stress matrix
- Reliability and confidence evidence
- Reproducible automated tests

## Expected impact to validate in field pilots

- Earlier leak and pump-failure detection
- Lower avoidable water loss
- Safer contamination isolation
- More precise irrigation
- Reduced drought-driven fire risk
- Resilient operation in low-connectivity regions
