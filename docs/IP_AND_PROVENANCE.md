## Reused engineering lineage

- **Black Dragon Studio:** simulation-first validation workflow and testing discipline.
- **Autex:** reliability, confidence and fail-safe decision-evidence patterns.

These projects were created by the submitting developer/team. AquaGuardian reorganizes and adapts selected concepts into a new water-domain closed-loop engineering prototype.
