# GPIW 2026 — Self-Assessment Against the 8 Official Criteria

Each criterion carries equal weight (Guidelines, Section 11). Self-scored
honestly — strengths and gaps both stated, since the Technical Jury will find
the gaps regardless.

| # | Criterion | Where AquaGuardian is strong | Where it is thin |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Technical Excellence** | Working closed-loop engine, reproducible tests, an independently built hardware simulator called live (not cited) for the edge-latency claim | No peer review, no third-party technical audit |
| 2 | **Scalability & Feasibility** | Architecture is domain-generalized — the aviation-domain reflex kernel and MEC cognitive engine are reused unmodified for water | No cost-of-integration analysis with real SCADA/utility systems yet |
| 3 | **Life-Cycle Impact** | Reduces avoidable water loss and irrigation waste; edge-first design avoids continuous cloud energy draw | No lifecycle/embodied-carbon analysis of the physical sensor/edge hardware itself |
| 4 | **Durability & Safety** | Explicit stress-testing under sensor noise and communication loss; hard vetoes on unsafe actions in the MEC layer | No field durability data; no independent security audit; threat model is documented but unverified |
| 5 | **Clear Market Need** | Beachhead and expansion segments defined with named buyer/user/approver roles; positioned as a layer, not a replacement | No signed letter of interest from a design partner yet |
| 6 | **Performance Measurability** | Every headline number is generated by code in the repo and reproducible via `pytest`; per-scenario JSON evidence, not slide claims | Metrics are simulated, not measured against real hydraulic infrastructure |
| 7 | **Audacious Yet Achievable Vision** | Clear TRL 3 → 4 → 5–6 roadmap with named, concrete next steps rather than a vague "scale up" claim | The vision (universal engineering platform beyond water) is intentionally not emphasized in this application to avoid overclaiming beyond the evidence |
| 8 | **Long-Term Viability & Execution Capacity** | Founder has four connected prior projects in the same validation-before-action pattern; capability gaps are named, not hidden | Solo team; no co-founder, no advisor, no funding history yet |

## What would most improve the score before September 30, 2026

In rough priority order:

1. **A signed letter of interest from a pilot host** (farm, irrigation
   district, or small utility) — directly strengthens Criteria 5 and 8, and
   is explicitly listed as still-open in `SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md`.
2. **The required 1–2 minute video** (`docs/GPIW_VIDEO_SCRIPT.md` has a
   draft script) — a hard submission requirement, not yet recorded.
3. **A named technical advisor or collaborator** (even informal) in a
   hydraulic-engineering or OT-security capacity — strengthens Criterion 8
   without requiring a real pilot yet.
4. **Live demo URL** — if the FastAPI console is deployed somewhere
   reachable (not just runnable locally), add it to the Supporting Evidence
   upload; it materially strengthens Criterion 6 (jurors can run scenarios
   themselves instead of trusting a video).

None of the above require inventing evidence that doesn't exist — they are
about closing gaps that are already accurately disclosed in the current
package.
