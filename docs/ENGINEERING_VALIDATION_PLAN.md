# Engineering Validation Plan

## PoC metrics

- Risk detection score
- Decision latency
- Percentage of stress profiles passed
- Water loss proxy
- Energy consumption proxy
- Service disruption
- Contamination exposure
- Crop stress
- Wildfire risk proxy

## Experimental comparison

Compare three policies:

1. Reactive baseline: act only after threshold breach
2. Detection-only AI: anomaly detection followed by fixed response
3. AquaGuardian closed loop: evaluate multiple responses in a digital twin

## Field validation pathway

1. Calibrate with historical utility or farm data
2. Hardware-in-the-loop test with pump, valves and sensors
3. Shadow-mode pilot with no autonomous actuation
4. Operator-reviewed recommendations
5. Limited certified control deployment

No percentage savings or real-world accuracy should be claimed until measured in a calibrated field pilot.
