# Model Card and Limitations

## Purpose
Demonstrate the architecture and measurable behavior of a closed-loop engineering decision process for water infrastructure.

## Evidence level
All values are generated by an included deterministic simulator. They are reproducible engineering hypotheses, not measured field outcomes.

## Baselines
- Reactive baseline: fixed emergency action after a modeled 300-second delay.
- Detection-only AI: rapid fixed rule with no candidate simulation or stress testing.
- AquaGuardian: evaluates candidates under nominal, noisy, severe/offline and extreme profiles.

## Limitations
The hydraulic, agronomic and fire models are simplified. Sensor calibration, real hydraulic network topology, weather uncertainty, actuator reliability, local regulation and human-in-the-loop procedures require field validation before deployment.

## Safe interpretation
Use results to compare software strategies inside this PoC only. Do not treat them as guaranteed savings, safety performance or deployment-ready control parameters.
